Type scale — Urbanist 300 / 400 / 500
Eleven steps, and nothing between them. Size and weight are never written at the call site — a component names the role the text plays (.t-body, .t-caption) and the scale decides how it looks. Weight descends as size does: the display line is the only 500 on a page, the money tiers are 400, and everything at body size and below is 300. Emphasis inside a step is still allowed — a name in a dense row takes 500 — but a size outside these eleven is not. Headings are separate complete classes and never take a step: .t-title 24/500 outranks .t-subtitle 20/500, which outranks .t-subhead 20/400 by weight alone.

Spacing
Density comes from a small card gap on a flat surface — never from a nested container. When there is little data, narrow the composition or stack it; do not stretch content to fill the width.
